Learn any song, together.

Tutti is a music-learning app for Android. Open a song and Tutti shows its chords, key, tempo, lyrics and sections, follows along while it plays, and gives every musician their own practice space — keys, guitar, bass, saxophone, voice and choir.

Chords that follow the song

See each chord as it happens, with the next ones coming up.

A page for every instrument

Guitar, bass and sax tabs, a tuner, and lessons for your own instrument.

Choir and harmony

Practise soprano, alto, tenor and bass parts with guide voices.

Song search and playback powered by YouTube

Tutti uses YouTube API Services to find a song’s video when you search for it, and plays videos with the official YouTube embedded player inside the app. Tutti does not download, copy or modify YouTube videos.

By using Tutti you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy, and how Tutti handles information in our Privacy Policy.
