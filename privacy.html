Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026
App: Tutti (Android)
Operator: Kwame Oti
Contact: tutti.app.help@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the Tutti app handles, why, where it goes, how long it is kept and how you can delete it.

YouTube API Services

Tutti uses YouTube API Services. By using Tutti you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube is a Google service; Google’s handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What Tutti accesses, uses and stores through YouTube API Services

  • Search words you type (for example a song title) are sent to YouTube to find matching videos.
  • Public video information returned by YouTube — video ID, title, channel name and thumbnail — is shown in the app so you can pick a video.
  • To avoid repeating the same search, Tutti’s server keeps search results for up to 24 hours, then deletes them automatically.
  • When you open a video as a song, its video ID and title are saved with that song’s project on your phone, so you can find it again. Suggested “similar songs” (titles, artists and video IDs) are also remembered on your phone so the list doesn’t disappear.
  • Videos play in the official YouTube embedded player. YouTube may collect information when you watch, as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What Tutti does not do

  • Tutti does not ask you to sign in with a Google or YouTube account and does not access your YouTube account, channel, history or subscriptions.
  • Tutti does not download, copy or modify YouTube videos, and does not sell or share YouTube data with anyone.

Because Tutti never connects to your Google account, there is no account access to revoke. If you ever grant any app access to your Google account, you can remove it at Google security settings.

1. Information you give Tutti

  • Songs and recordings you choose to import or record (audio or video files, microphone recordings, practice takes).
  • Your edits — chord or lyric corrections, section names, loop points, notes and settings.
  • Optional profile details — a display name, picture and instrument. These stay on your phone.
  • Questions you ask Tutti’s AI helper, typed or spoken.

2. Information Tutti creates

To help you learn a song, Tutti works out its tempo, key, chords, sections, melody and bass notes, lyrics with timings, separated instrument and voice tracks, harmony guides, practice charts and sheet music.

3. Where your information goes

  • Your phone. Projects, settings, profile and progress are stored on your phone.
  • Tutti’s analysis server. When you ask Tutti to analyse a song, the audio is sent to Tutti’s server, which creates the analysis and separated tracks and keeps them so the song can be reopened and played.
  • AI service providers. For lyrics, spoken questions, read-aloud answers and AI music features, short pieces of audio or text are sent to AI providers (such as OpenAI and ElevenLabs) only to produce that result. Their keys are kept on Tutti’s server, never in the app.
  • YouTube, as described in the YouTube API Services section above.
  • When you choose to share. Tutti shares, prints or exports only when you tap those actions.

Tutti does not sell personal information, does not show ads and contains no advertising or tracking software.

4. How long information is kept

  • Information on your phone stays until you delete the song or project, clear the app’s data, or uninstall Tutti.
  • YouTube search results on Tutti’s server are deleted automatically after 24 hours.
  • Songs analysed on Tutti’s server and their tracks are kept so you can reopen them, until you ask us to delete them.

5. Deleting your information

  • Delete a song or project in the app, or uninstall Tutti, to remove what is stored on your phone.
  • To delete anything stored on Tutti’s server, email tutti.app.help@gmail.com with the song names. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

6. Permissions

  • Microphone — only when you record, use the tuner, “hear and correct”, or ask a spoken question.
  • Files and photos — only the files or picture you pick.

Tutti does not use your location, contacts or advertising ID.

7. Children

Tutti is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

10. Contact
